# Prompts and art direction

## Character concept prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production character turnaround sheet for the survival game 1079. A fictional adult winter expedition hiker, late-1950s-inspired practical clothing, not any real historical person. Three full-body orthographic views of exactly the same character: front, left side, back, consistent proportions and equipment, neutral relaxed A-pose, boots on same baseline. Mature restrained low-poly 3D art with large angular silhouettes, two tonal values per material, no photorealism, no cartoon baby proportions. Thick muted slate-blue canvas hooded anorak to upper thighs, dark charcoal wool trousers, bulky felt boots with dark soles, simple mittens, beige wool scarf partially covering face, deep hood with small visible face and dark inner lining. Olive canvas external-frame rucksack with leather straps, rolled gray blanket tied across its top, modest metal cup hanging at side. No weapons, no modern synthetic tactical equipment, no logos. Cold blue-gray palette, subdued tan leather details, soft cool studio lighting. Clean pale gray paper background, generous spacing between three figures, readable geometry useful for building a real-time model. Small heading only “1079 / CHARACTER 01”. No other text, no scenery.

## Direction from Anton

Large silhouettes, stylised flat geometry, two tonal values per surface, washed snow, blue shadows, and warm light only from fire or lamp. Adult, dry and quiet. Avoid comedy, monsters presented as fact, gore, and unsupported historical certainty. The survival cast is fictional; real names and historical points belong to archive mode.
